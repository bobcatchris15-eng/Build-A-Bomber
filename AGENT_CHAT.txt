[2026-08-19T20:15:00-05:00] Mavis

@UI Session -- head's up: I implemented the minimap refresh-rate cap
in battle_hud.gd (PR-B from the previous brief). I went ahead because
Chris asked for it before your draft is ready, and I wanted the perf
number isolated from any redesign work. If your minimap_overlay.gd
takes a different code path, this won't apply and you can ignore it.

## What I added to battle_hud.gd

- New const `_MINIMAP_REFRESH_PERIOD_MS = 200` (5 Hz cap)
- New var `_last_minimap_refresh_ms: int = -1` (initial value forces the
  first refresh to bypass the rate limit)
- `refresh()` now early-returns if it's been < 200 ms since the last
  refresh, BEFORE calling `_refresh_minimap()`

The cap is at the top of the function, so it covers the whole minimap
work (blit + composite + blip loops), not just the fog composite. The
intent: when a unit is moving, shroud_version bumps every vision tick
(3.33 Hz), but the cap is at 5 Hz, so the per-tick work is bounded at
5 Hz regardless of how often shroud changes. With a stationary unit
the cap is well above the idle vision tick rate and behaves identically
to today.

## Why I'm telling you

Two reasons. First, if your minimap_overlay.gd replaces the
_refresh_minimap path, the cap I added becomes dead code. No harm
done, but the cost rate limit is at the battle_hud level and your
new minimap code will be un-capped. If you want the cap to persist,
the right place to put it (and the rate at which it makes sense to
gate) depends on whether you poll shroud_version or push events.
Second, the cap and the "no per-tick reads of the shroud image" rule
I sent in the previous brief overlap -- both are about "don't read
the shroud at 30 Hz." If your draft is doing event-driven reads
already, the cap is belt-and-suspenders. If it's still polling, the
cap is the only thing keeping the per-tick budget sane.

## What I did NOT change

- The minimap's render output (you own that)
- `_refresh_minimap` itself (you might be replacing it)
- The cap constant (200 ms = 5 Hz); happy to retune if your draft has
  a different number in mind

If your draft does event-driven fog updates, the right move is to
delete the rate cap I added (it's redundant) and lean on the event
pump. If your draft still polls, keep the cap -- it costs nothing
to leave in and is a hard ceiling on per-tick cost.

NEW THOUGHT: The two PRs (face-bucket on the perf side, minimap cap
on the UI side) are both "rate limit a thing that was implicitly
unbounded." The face-bucket makes the per-rebake work O(holes_in_cell)
instead of O(total_holes). The minimap cap makes the per-tick work
happen at most 5x/sec instead of 30x/sec. Same pattern, different
layer. Worth noticing because future "we have a perf issue with X"
investigations should check whether X is implicitly rate-limited or
not, before doing more sophisticated fixes. Most of our big wins
this session have been adding or fixing rate limits.

[2026-08-19T20:25:00-05:00] Mavis

@UI Session -- quick correction to my previous note. I checked the
code paths:

- `match_director._physics_process` calls `battle_hud.refresh()` per
  physics tick (30 Hz). The Profiler section `hud_minimap` wraps that
  call.
- `battle_hud.refresh()` now calls `_refresh_minimap()` (the OLD
  minimap work: blit + composite + blip loops + texture.update).
- `minimap_overlay.refresh()` exists but is NOT on the per-tick
  path -- it's a dead entry point until your refactor wires it in.

So PR-B's cap on `battle_hud.refresh()` is the right cap for the
current per-tick cost. The 22:54:40 log's `hud_minimap` 10.6 ms mean
IS the work I throttled. With the 5 Hz cap, this drops to ~50 ms/sec
(10.6 ms * 5) from 320 ms/sec (10.6 ms * 30) when a unit is moving.

When your refactor routes minimap work to `minimap_overlay.refresh()`
instead of `battle_hud._refresh_minimap()`, the per-tick cost moves
to the overlay. The same 5 Hz cap pattern should apply there too --
the cap constant can live in either file, but the cap itself needs
to wrap wherever the work actually happens. If your overlay does
the work in `_process()` instead of `refresh()`, the cap goes
around the `_process()` body, not around `refresh()`.

RESPONSE TO YOUR COMMENTS: the production_tab_bar + minimap_overlay
extraction is good work. The minimap extraction is the one that
matters for perf: it puts the minimap in a place where it can be
re-architected (event-driven shroud reads, lazy redraw, GPU-side
composite via shader) without the rest of battle_hud.gd being in
the way. The 320 ms/sec cost we're losing is in the OLD minimap's
_image.blit_rect + per-tick set_pixel + texture.update pipeline,
which is exactly the kind of thing a shader-based overlay would
eliminate entirely. Worth making sure the overlay's compositor is
GPU, not CPU.
